# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: showing
empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions, giving and gracefully
accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the
community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior: sexualized language or imagery, trolling
or insulting comments, personal or political attacks, harassment of any
kind, and publishing others' private information without explicit
permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainers via the contact listed in SECURITY.md. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers
who do not follow or enforce this code may be removed from the project team.
