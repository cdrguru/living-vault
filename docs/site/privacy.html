Legal

Privacy policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026. Controller: ITSesh LLC, Florida, United States. Contact: pdavis@itsesh.com.

The Living Vault is a local-first monitor for reusable agentic assets. This policy explains the limited information ITSesh processes for Microsoft Marketplace commerce and support. The detailed product telemetry boundary is described in the telemetry guide and summarized here.

Information processed

The product’s local collectors process structural metadata such as asset, tool, harness, and model identifiers; timestamps; invocation and error counts; durations; and token totals where available. Collectors do not read prompt text, responses, reasoning, command text, tool arguments or results, file contents, file paths, summaries, titles, or authentication tokens.

ITSesh’s Marketplace service processes the purchased subscription identifier, offer and plan identifiers, subscription status, lifecycle operation identifiers, purchaser tenant identifiers supplied by Microsoft, and operational timestamps. Support requests contain the information the requester chooses to send.

Purposes and legal basis

ITSesh uses commerce records to resolve and activate purchases, keep access aligned with Microsoft’s subscription state, prevent duplicate processing, troubleshoot failures, and meet accounting or legal obligations. Support information is used to answer requests and protect the service. Processing is necessary to perform the subscription agreement and for legitimate security and operational interests.

Storage and sharing

Real product telemetry stays on the customer’s machine by default. Public demos and distributable builds use synthetic data. Optional Fabric imports go to the customer-selected Fabric environment only after an explicit push. ITSesh does not sell personal data.

Commerce records are hosted in Microsoft Azure in the United States. Microsoft processes Marketplace transaction and billing information under the customer’s Microsoft agreement. ITSesh may use infrastructure and support providers only as needed to operate the service or comply with law.

Retention and deletion

Local product data remains until the customer deletes it. ITSesh retains operational subscription records while the subscription is active and for up to 24 months afterward unless a longer period is required for tax, fraud prevention, dispute, or legal obligations. Eligible deletion or access requests may be sent to pdavis@itsesh.com and will be handled after identity verification.

Security

ITSesh uses HTTPS, service-to-service OAuth, Azure Key Vault for secret storage accessed through a managed identity, webhook signature and claim validation, idempotent lifecycle processing, and separation between commerce data and product telemetry. No system is completely secure; suspected incidents should be reported privately to pdavis@itsesh.com.

Children and changes

The service is intended for business and professional users and is not directed to children. Material policy changes will be posted with a revised effective date.
